Coconut know-how

How to tell a good coconut by ear

You cannot see inside a coconut, but you can hear it. Knock two coconuts together and listen. We made this short video to share a piece of knowledge every coconut buyer should have.

APPROVED

A clear, solid sound: it is a good coconut.

REJECTED

A dull or different sound: it is not a good coconut.

Business opportunity

Four businesses you can start with our coconuts

Whether you run a company or want to start small at home, a coconut business begins with a good coconut. Here is what each business needs, and why Mozambican coconut is the right raw material.

What we sell: fresh mature coconuts, copra and virgin coconut oil. The four businesses below are ideas for what you can make with them; we supply the raw material.

01

Virgin coconut oil

Press the dried kernel and bottle the oil for cooking, skin and hair.

You need
Grater, dryer, oil press, filter, bottles
How it is made
Open the coconuts, grate the kernel, dry it at low heat, press it, let the oil settle, filter and bottle.
02

Coconut butter

Grind dried coconut into a smooth spread.

You need
Dryer, grinder, jars
How it is made
Dry the grated kernel, then grind it until it turns into a smooth, creamy paste. Fill the jars while it is still soft.
03

Desiccated coconut

Grate, dry and pack the kernel for bakeries and food makers.

You need
Grater, dryer, sieve, bag sealer
How it is made
Remove the brown skin, grate the white kernel, dry it until crisp, sieve it by size and seal it in bags.
04

Coconut charcoal

Turn the empty shells into charcoal for grills and briquettes.

You need
Drum kiln, crusher, bags
How it is made
Dry the empty shells, burn them slowly in a closed drum with little air, let them cool, then crush and bag.

One coconut, several products: the kernel for oil, butter or desiccated coconut, and the shell for charcoal.

Garage guide

What you need to make virgin coconut oil in your garage

A small oil business fits in a garage. This is the basic setup; sizes and brands depend on your budget.

Equipment

  • A clean table and a tool to crack the coconuts
  • An electric coconut grater
  • A tray dryer or low-heat oven
  • A small screw oil press
  • Food-grade buckets and filter cloth
  • Glass bottles or jars, labels and a scale
  • Clean water and good hygiene

The process

  1. Open the coconuts and remove the kernel.
  2. Grate the kernel.
  3. Dry it at low heat until crisp.
  4. Press it to extract the oil.
  5. Let the oil settle, then filter it.
  6. Bottle, label and sell.

As a rough guide, 12 to 15 mature coconuts give about 1 litre of oil, depending on size and method.

Check the food-safety rules in your country before you sell.
